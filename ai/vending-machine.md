# Vending machine: a gumball machine as a first business

In 2023, when I was 13, I tried the gumball machine idea. It was one of many ideas that summer, and I jumped to a new idea every few days. I have no record of a machine in a shop or a single coin: **0 euros earned.** Later I wrote one summary for all my 2023 tests: nothing worked, and together they cost me about minus 160 euros.
