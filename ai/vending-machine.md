# Vending machine: a gumball machine as a first business

I tried the gumball machine idea on 25 June 2023, when I was 13. It was one of many ideas that summer: printed T-shirts three days before, Amazon Influencer two days after, tennis balls a week later. I have no record of a machine in a shop or a single coin: **0 euros earned.**

Later I wrote one summary for all my 2023 tests: nothing worked, and together they cost me about minus 160 euros. The only things that worked were YouTube, mowing lawns and selling old stuff on eBay, all a lot of work.

What I learned: I jumped to a new idea every few days. If I tried again, I would first get one shop owner to say yes, count the people walking past, and only then buy one machine and test it for at least 8 weeks.
