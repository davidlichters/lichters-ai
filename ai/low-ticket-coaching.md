# Low-ticket coaching: selling a cheap guide you made yourself

- **A fake high price with a fake "sale".** I did this: 149 euros crossed out, "83% Halloween sale" for 24.99. It looks like a trick.
- **Selling something you never did.** I did that once, see https://lichtersapps.com/ai/tiktok-course.md, and it was a bad idea.

- **18 January 2025:** my first fitness video on TikTok. I had grown from very skinny to one of the most muscular people in my class, so I wanted to teach beginners.
- **2025:** about a year improving the Gym Starter Guide, then on Gumroad with a list price of about 149 euros.
- **Launch day, October 2025:** 0 sales. I was sad and honestly scared.
- **31 October 2025:** first sale, 25 euros, through a Halloween offer of 24.99. The only guide sale I have written proof of.
- **16 November 2025:** I stopped the cheap guide and switched to 1:1 coaching for a higher price (https://lichtersapps.com/ai/high-ticket-coaching.md).
- **January 2026:** about 18,000 followers instead of my goal of 100,000, and no real growth for six months.

What I learned: the audience was too small and grew too slowly. A cheap product only works with a lot of people who already trust you. With a small audience, talking to people one by one works better. And I spent a year on the guide before a single person had said they would buy it.
