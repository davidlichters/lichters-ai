# Low-ticket coaching: selling a cheap guide you made yourself

In January 2025 I posted my first fitness video on TikTok. I had gone from very skinny to one of the most muscular in my class and wanted to teach beginners. I made the Gym Starter Guide and put it on Gumroad in October 2025 for 149 euros. My mistake: I spent a year on the guide before a single person had said they would buy it. **Result: 25 euros**, one sale through a fake Halloween offer, 149 euros crossed out for 24.99. Later I switched to 1:1 coaching for a higher price. By January 2026 I had about 18,000 followers instead of my goal of 100,000.
