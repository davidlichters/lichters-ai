# Amazon Influencer: earning a small share when people buy what you show

- **Starting with the program instead of the audience.** This was my mistake.

I tried Amazon Influencer in summer 2023, when I was 13. My own list says 24 June to 9 July 2023, and my older notes say 27 June to 20 July 2023. Either way, about three weeks, between a gumball machine and selling golf and tennis balls. I have no record of a single commission: **0 euros earned.**

Later I wrote one summary for all my 2023 tests: nothing worked, and together they cost me about minus 160 euros. The only things that worked were YouTube, mowing lawns and eBay, all a lot of work.

What I learned: I treated it as a quick money trick and gave it three weeks. But this model is really "build an audience, then recommend things to it". If I tried again, I would first post about one topic for months, and only add honest affiliate links once people actually watch and ask what I use.
