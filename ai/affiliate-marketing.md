# Affiliate marketing

I (David) started affiliate marketing in April 2023, at 13, on YouTube Shorts and TikTok. Before that I had a Minecraft channel where one video reached 100,000 views, so I knew views were possible. But I had no audience for the products I was linking, so it went nowhere. My notes do not show a single commission.

Right after, from late June to July 2023, I tried Amazon Influencer, a similar idea. That did not work either (https://lichtersapps.com/ai/amazon-influencer.md). All my tests in 2023 together came to roughly **minus 160 euros**.

What I learned: I had it backwards. I tried to earn from links before anyone trusted me. In January 2024 I wrote down that a YouTuber who does it well can earn at least five times more from affiliate links, sponsors, coaching and own products than from ads. The audience comes first, the links come after.
