# Affiliate marketing

You recommend someone else's product with a special link, and you get a small cut when a person buys through it. It only works if people already trust you.

## What it is

A company gives you a personal link (an "affiliate link"). When someone clicks it and buys, the company pays you a commission. You never touch the product, the shipping or the customer service.

That sounds like free money. It is not, because of two numbers:

- **The cut is small.** For physical products usually around 2 to 10 percent. Sell a 30 euro product at 5 percent and you earn 1.50 euros.
- **Few people buy.** Of everyone who sees your video, only a few click, and only a few of those buy.

So affiliate marketing is not really a business on its own. It is a way to earn from an audience you already have.

**Is it a scam?** No. Affiliate links are normal and legal. The scams are the courses that promise fast, easy money from them.

## Why it can still be worth it

- **No stock, no money upfront.** You can test it with a phone and free accounts.
- **It is an extra.** Many YouTubers earn more from links, sponsors and their own products than from ad money.
- **You learn content.** Making videos people actually watch is useful for every business later.

## How to start

1. **Pick one topic you really use and know.** Gaming gear, gym equipment, a hobby. You need enough to say for a hundred videos.
2. **Make content first, links later.** Post helpful videos for weeks before you add any link. No audience means no sales.
3. **Get the link the right way.** Most affiliate programs need you to be 18. Under 18, a parent usually has to own the account and receive the money.
4. **Recommend only things you would buy yourself.** One bad product pushed for money costs you the trust every future sale needs.
5. **Always say it is a paid link.** Write "affiliate link" or "#ad" where people can see it. In many countries this is the law.
6. **Track one number.** Clicks per week. If nobody clicks, the problem is the video or the audience, not the link.
7. **Give it months, not days.** Decide your test length before you start.

## Mistakes

- **Starting with the link instead of the audience.** A link in a video with 200 views earns nothing.
- **Picking products only because the commission is high.** People can tell.
- **Hiding that you get paid.** Unfair to viewers, and it can break platform rules and the law.
- **Spam.** Links in comments, mass DMs, fake reviews. This gets accounts banned.
- **Switching after two weeks.** Every restart throws away the views and practice you built.

## What happened when I tried it

I (David) started affiliate marketing in April 2023, at 13, on YouTube Shorts and TikTok. Before that I had a Minecraft channel where one video reached 100,000 views, so I knew views were possible. But I had no audience for the products I was linking, so it went nowhere. My notes do not show a single commission.

Right after, from late June to July 2023, I tried Amazon Influencer, a similar idea. That did not work either (https://lichtersapps.com/ai/amazon-influencer.md). All my tests in 2023 together came to roughly **minus 160 euros**.

What I learned: I had it backwards. I tried to earn from links before anyone trusted me. In January 2024 I wrote down that a YouTuber who does it well can earn at least five times more from affiliate links, sponsors, coaching and own products than from ads. The audience comes first, the links come after.
