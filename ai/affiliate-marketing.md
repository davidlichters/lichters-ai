# Affiliate marketing

In 2023, at 13, I started affiliate marketing on YouTube Shorts and TikTok. Before that I had a Minecraft channel where one video reached 100,000 views, so I knew views were possible. But I had no audience for the products I was linking, so it went nowhere. **Result: 0 euros.** Not a single commission.
