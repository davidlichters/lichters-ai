# Start a gaming YouTube channel

I (David) started a Minecraft YouTube channel in October 2022, when I was 12. It was my first real attempt to make money online. Around the same time I also offered Minecraft-related work on Fiverr, and that did not work.

One video, uploaded on 11 March 2023, had **100,000 views** by 13 April 2023. I stopped the channel on 14 April 2023, the day after, and moved on to affiliate marketing. My notes do not say how much, if anything, the channel earned.

Looking back:

- **One big video is not a business.** The smarter move would have been to study why that video worked and make more like it instead of switching.
- **Switching kept me at zero.** In 2023 I tried more than ten ideas. Together they ended at roughly minus 160 euros.
- **Video stayed part of my life.** Later I ran a self-improvement YouTube channel, and a fitness TikTok account I started in January 2025 had about 18,000 followers by January 2026.
- **Views and money are different goals.** In September 2026 I wrote that YouTube brings almost no money and that forcing yourself to post eats your focus. Views help most when they lead people to something you actually offer.
