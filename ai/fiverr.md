# Fiverr: selling a skill online

In 2023 I clicked on a video called "How To Make Money on Fiverr Without Skills". That title says everything about my mistake. I had no skill strangers would pay for, and I hoped the platform would do the work. My gig was around Minecraft, because that was what I knew.

**Result: 0 euros.** No sales that I have any record of.

Later, when I was running a business, I became a buyer on Fiverr myself and paid freelancers for tasks that weren't worth my time. I could do them myself, but it was faster to work on my business and pay a freelancer with that money.
