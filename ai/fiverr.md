# Fiverr: selling a skill online

Fiverr was my first try at making money online, from 17 May 2022 to 9 April 2023. On 23 January 2023 I clicked on a video called "How To Make Money on Fiverr Without Skills". That title says everything about my mistake. I had no skill strangers would pay for, and I hoped the platform would do the work. My gig was around Minecraft, because that was what I knew.

**Result: 0 euros.** No sales that I have any record of.

Later, when I was running a business, I became a buyer on Fiverr myself and paid freelancers for tasks I could not do well. Then it made sense. Buyers do not pay for "someone who wants to earn money". They pay for a clear result, shown in samples, from someone already good at it.

If I tried again, I would first spend a few months getting really good at one thing like thumbnails or video editing, make 10 strong samples, and only then open a narrow gig.
