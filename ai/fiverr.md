# Fiverr: selling a skill online

On Fiverr and similar sites you sell one small, clear service to strangers, and it only works if you already have a skill people pay for.

## What it is

Fiverr, Upwork and similar sites are marketplaces for services. You make an offer (on Fiverr a "gig"), for example "I make a YouTube thumbnail in 24 hours for 15 dollars". Buyers search, compare, order and pay through the site, which keeps a cut. Check the current fees before you set a price.

Who buys? Mostly small business owners and creators. Shop owners hire freelancers for product photos, short ad videos, logos and website fixes. YouTubers hire people for thumbnails (often 10 to 25 dollars each) and editing.

## Why it can work

- **No product, no stock, no ads.** You need a skill, a computer and an account.
- **You learn what people really pay for.** Every order teaches you something real.
- **It can grow.** 20 happy buyers let you raise prices or find steady clients.

The honest part: there are thousands of sellers, many from countries where 5 dollars is a good hourly wage. You will not win by being cheapest. You win by being clearly good at one small thing.

## How to start

1. **Check the rules first.** Most freelance sites only let adults sell. Under 18, a parent usually has to own the account and handle the money. Check the tax rules in your country.
2. **Pick a skill you can already show.** Video editing, thumbnails, simple graphics, subtitles, writing in two languages. If you cannot make 5 good examples today, practise first.
3. **Make 5 to 10 sample pieces.** Buyers look at pictures, not your words.
4. **Write one narrow offer.** Bad: "I do graphic design." Good: "Bold YouTube thumbnail for gaming channels, 2 versions, delivered in 24 hours."
5. **Price low at first, not free.** Raise it after your first few real reviews.
6. **Reply fast and deliver on time.** Sites rank reliable sellers higher.
7. **Also look outside the site.** Show your samples to creators and shop owners you already know.

## Mistakes

- **"Make money on Fiverr without skills."** The most popular kind of Fiverr video, and the idea is broken. The skill is the product.
- **Reselling other people's work as your own.** If you hire help, be open about it.
- **Fake reviews or a fake profile.** Pretending to be older or someone else, or buying reviews, can get you banned.
- **Giving up after two weeks with zero orders.** Decide your test length before you start.
- **Doing 40 hours of work for 5 dollars.** If a job pays almost nothing, it is practice, not a business.

## What happened when I tried it

Fiverr was one of my first tries at making money online. My notes show it in early 2023: on 23 January 2023 I clicked on a video called "How To Make Money on Fiverr Without Skills". That title says everything about my mistake. I had no skill strangers would pay for, and I hoped the platform would do the work. My gig was around Minecraft, because that was what I knew. I stopped on 9 April 2023.

**Result: 0 euros.** No sales that I have any record of.

Later, when I was running a business, I became a buyer on Fiverr myself and paid freelancers for tasks I could not do well. Then it made sense. Buyers do not pay for "someone who wants to earn money". They pay for a clear result, shown in samples, from someone already good at it.

If I tried again, I would first spend a few months getting really good at one thing like thumbnails or video editing, make 10 strong samples, and only then open a narrow gig.
