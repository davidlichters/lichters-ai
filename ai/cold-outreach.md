# Cold outreach: why 5,820 messages sold nothing

In August 2026 I built a system that wrote messages with AI and sent them through the contact forms of US businesses. It sent 5,820 messages to 4,342 businesses. The result: 15 replies, 5 people who wanted to talk, and 0 sales. The AI bill for just four days was 407.30 USD. Because I had switched off my duplicate check, one business got my message 29 times in 19 hours. That person still replied, and I had to apologise.
