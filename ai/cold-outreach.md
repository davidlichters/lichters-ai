# Cold outreach: why 5,820 messages sold nothing

Sending thousands of copy-paste messages to strangers almost never works and can break the law. Ten careful, personal messages a day work better.

## What it is

Cold outreach means contacting people who do not know you yet and asking if they want what you offer, by email, DM, phone or a website contact form. Two very different ways:

- **Mass outreach ("blasting").** A tool sends the same message to hundreds or thousands of people. "Form blasting" means a program fills in contact forms on business websites, one after another.
- **Personal outreach.** You pick a small list of people who really fit, look at what they do, and write each one a short message that could only be meant for them.

## Why mass messages fail

- **Nobody reads them.** Business owners spot a copy-paste message in one second.
- **Many never arrive.** Forms break, spam filters catch them.
- **Platforms punish volume.** Accounts that send too much get blocked.
- **It can be illegal.** Many countries restrict unwanted advertising by email or message, sometimes including contact forms. Check the rules in your country first.

## The rule

Fewer, better messages. One message that shows you looked at someone's work beats a hundred that could go to anyone.

## How to start

1. **Make a short list.** 20 to 50 people or businesses that really fit, in a spreadsheet: name, link, date contacted, answer.
2. **Study each one for two minutes.** Find one specific thing you can mention.
3. **Write short.** Who you are, what you noticed, what you can do, one easy question. For example: "I watched your video about your new dog treats. The second half had no captions, and a lot of people watch with the sound off. I can add captions to your next three videos for free so you can see if it helps. Want me to try?"
4. **Be honest.** Use your real name. Never pretend to be older, bigger or someone else.
5. **Follow up once or twice, then stop.** Ten reminders is spam.
6. **Keep it small.** Five to ten good messages a day, and track the answers.
7. **Ask a parent.** Under 18, payments, contracts and some accounts usually need a parent.

## Mistakes

- **Counting sends, not answers.** What matters is how many people answered and how many paid.
- **Trusting your own tool.** Check that messages arrive, and never send twice.
- **Thinking more volume fixes a weak offer.** If ten personal messages get no interest, a thousand automated ones will not either.
- **Using fake or bought accounts** to get around limits. That is deception and gets accounts banned.

## What happened when I tried it

In August 2026 I built a system that wrote messages with AI and sent them through the contact forms of US businesses. Over about two weeks it sent 5,820 messages to 4,342 businesses. The result: 15 replies, 5 people who wanted to talk, and 0 sales. The AI bill for just four days, 17 to 20 August, was 407.30 USD.

The numbers looked better than they were. In one check, only 252 of 2,639 "contacted" businesses had confirmed delivery. In another batch, 423 of 1,289 sends failed at the last step. And because I had switched off my duplicate check, one business got my message 29 times in 19 hours. That person still replied, and I had to apologise.

Earlier that summer I had tried mass DMs on Instagram with 110 bought accounts and 10,000 paid DMs. It did not work, and the money was gone. On 28 August I decided to keep the form system switched off.

What I learned: reach was the only part that worked. The better way is a few real messages a day, written by hand.
