# Mentors and Paid Communities

A mentor can save you months, but only if you bring a real project, real numbers and real questions. Watching or paying is not the same as doing.

## What it is

Three levels of learning from someone ahead of you:

1. **Free content.** YouTube videos, podcasts, posts.
2. **Paid community or course.** A group chat, a video library, sometimes group calls. Usually a few dollars to a few hundred.
3. **1:1 mentor or "high ticket" coaching.** Private calls with one person. Often 1,000 euros or more for about three months.

## Why it can help, and why it often doesn't

- A good mentor sees in five minutes what you would need five weeks to notice.
- But most people who buy a program never finish it, and the seller gets paid either way.
- Many mentors make most of their money from selling mentoring. "Sell a course" works for them because they already have a big audience. You do not.

## How to start

1. **Start free.** Pick one or two creators who actually did the exact thing you want. Do one thing from each video the same day.
2. **Write down why.** Before paying, write: "I am paying for X so that I can do Y by date Z." If you cannot fill it in, do not pay.
3. **Pay only for a skill gap you can name.** Good: "My videos get 200 views and I do not know why." Bad: "I want to be rich."
4. **Check the person.** Did they do the thing themselves?
5. **Keep it small first.** One cheap course or one single call tells you if their style fits.
6. **Prepare every call.** Bring your numbers and three exact questions. Act on the answers within 24 hours.
7. **Under 18?** Payments and contracts usually need a parent. Talk to them before you sign or send money.

## Warning signs

- "Only today", "price goes up Friday", countdown timers.
- Promises like "make your money back in 30 days".
- You feel pushed to borrow money.

## Mistakes

- **Copying the mentor's business instead of their first steps.** A beginner should first sell one small thing to one person.
- **Consuming instead of doing.** One hour of watching should give at least one hour of working.
- **Buying a mentor to feel safe.** If you are scared to post or sell, only posting and selling fixes that.

## What happened when I tried it

On 25 August 2023, when I was 13, I watched a four hour video by **Hamza Ahmed**, a YouTuber who talks about self improvement and business. Until about March 2024 I watched him all the time, by my own count around 1,300 of his hour long videos.

What helped: real habits. Plan tomorrow in the evening, train hard, sleep at the same time in a cool room (see https://lichtersapps.com/ai/sleep.md).

What did not help: I copied his business, not his start. In December 2023 I made a self improvement YouTube channel in his style and wanted to sell a course like him, with no audience and no product. It made 0 euros.

Later, when I was coaching people in fitness (https://lichtersapps.com/ai/high-ticket-coaching.md), one coach offered me group coaching for 1,000 euros plus another 1,000 once I made money with his help. At the end of 2025 I agreed to pay another coach around 1,400 euros for 1:1 mentoring. After about one and a half calls I stopped. I already knew most of what he said, I was just not doing it. I paid around 300 euros for those calls. In January 2026 I was about 1,100 euros in the minus overall.

My honest result: years of watching mentors gave me good habits and 0 euros of profit. The money only ever came when I sold something myself.
