# Mentors and Paid Communities

In 2023, when I was 13, I watched a four hour video by **Hamza Ahmed**. I watched him all the time, by my own count around 1,300 of his hour long videos. I copied his business, not his start. In December 2023 I made a self improvement YouTube channel in his style and wanted to sell a course like him, with no audience and no product. It made 0 euros. Later, when I was coaching people in fitness, I agreed to pay a coach around 1,400 euros for 1:1 mentoring. After about one and a half calls I stopped. I paid around 300 euros for those calls.
