# High-ticket coaching

High-ticket coaching means selling one expensive program (often 1,000 EUR or more) on a video call. It only works if you already have real results to show. I tried it for nine months and ended near zero.

## What it is

You help people reach one clear goal, like "gain your first 5 kg of muscle in 12 weeks". People find you through videos, message you, book a call, and you sell on that call. Four steps:

1. **Content:** short videos that show you know the topic.
2. **Messages (DMs):** you ask questions and find out if the person really fits.
3. **Call booking:** the person picks a time and shows up.
4. **The sales call:** you listen, show the plan, and ask for a yes.

## Why it is hard

- **Trust is the product.** Nobody pays 1,400 EUR without proof: real clients with real before-and-after results.
- **One call, at most one sale.**
- **People don't show up.** Many who book never come.
- **A yes is not money.** People say yes on the call and then never pay.
- **Money and contracts.** Under 18, payment accounts and contracts usually need your parents.

## How to start

1. **Get results first.** Coach 3 to 5 people for free or cheap. Write down their numbers each week. That is your proof.
2. **Pick one person and one result.** "Skinny teen guys who want to gain muscle before summer" beats "everyone who wants to be fit".
3. **Post short videos every day** that answer real questions from that person.
4. **In messages, ask before you explain.** How often do you train? What is your goal? Where are you stuck? Give one free tip, then offer a short call.
5. **Make showing up easy.** Confirm right after booking, remind them the day before and one hour before.
6. **Run the call in three parts.** Ask where they are and what stopped them. Show your plan for exactly their problem. Then ask clearly if they want to start.
7. **Get paid before you start coaching.**
8. **Track every number:** messages, calls booked, calls showed, yes, paid.

## Mistakes

- **Raising the price before having results.** Proof first, price second.
- **Fake proof.** A made-up coach persona, invented numbers, fake "only 2 slots left". It is lying. Show only what is true, even if it is small.
- **Buying a mentor to fix a missing product.** If nobody buys at 50 EUR, nobody buys at 1,400 EUR.
- **Counting a yes as money.** Only paid money counts.

## What happened when I tried it

Before this I posted fitness videos from January 2025 and sold a cheap fitness guide from October 2025 (https://lichtersapps.com/ai/low-ticket-coaching.md). From 17 November 2025 to 28 August 2026 I sold 1:1 and then high-ticket coaching.

- **Cheap 1:1 coaching first.** 60 EUR per week. By 26 December 2025 I had earned 374 EUR in total. In February 2026 two clients paid 25 EUR and 250 EUR.
- **High-ticket.** Prices around 1,400 to 2,500 EUR. My first big closed deal in March 2026 was 1,400 EUR. The person agreed, then stopped answering. I received 0 EUR.
- **Mentor.** I signed up with a paid mentor for 1,400 EUR. I paid part of it and the rest turned into debt.
- **Automation.** In March 2026 I paid for ManyChat (about 15 per month) and n8n (60 EUR per month) to answer DMs with AI. I cancelled ManyChat in August 2026.
- **Result.** In May 2026 my own note said "no sales to date" for the high-ticket offer. After a year I wrote that I went "from 0 to 0".
- **What I did wrong.** I also ran an Instagram account with a made-up coach persona and fake numbers. It cost more than it brought. Don't do that.

Lesson: results first, proof second, high price last.
