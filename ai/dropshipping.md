# Dropshipping: an online shop where someone else ships

Dropshipping is a paid-ads business: you spend money on ads before you know if anyone buys, so do the math first and expect your first product to fail.

## What it is

You build an online shop, usually on Shopify, and list a product you don't own. When someone buys, a supplier ships it straight to the customer. You keep the difference. Almost all small shops get buyers from paid ads, mostly on Facebook and Instagram (Meta).

## Why it is hard

- **Ads cost money every day**, not per sale. A product can burn $100 and sell nothing.
- **Margins are thin.** Product, shipping, fees and ads all come out of one price.
- **You depend on others.** Suppliers answer slowly, ad accounts get blocked, late packages turn into refunds.

## Under 18: read this first

Shopify Payments, Stripe and Meta ad accounts normally need an adult owner, and payouts go to an adult's bank account. A parent has to own the accounts and agree to the money risk. Never enter a fake birthday.

## How to start

1. **Do the math first.** Price minus product cost, shipping and about 3 to 5 percent payment fees is the most one sale may cost in ads. Example: a $69.99 bed with $47 left means any sale costing more than $47 in ads loses money.
2. **Pick one product** with a clear "wow" in a 3-second video, a price people pay without long thinking (often $30 to $80), not sold in every supermarket.
3. **Ask the supplier in writing:** days until delivery, tracking within 48 hours, what happens to lost packages.
4. **Build a simple, honest shop.** One product page, good photos, clear price, delivery time and refund policy. No fake timers, no fake "only 2 left", no fake reviews.
5. **Make many creatives.** The ad picture or video decides almost everything. Start with 10 to 20, built around 3 different messages (comfort, gift, looks cute).
6. **Start small and wait.** Let ads run 2 to 3 days. Touch nothing in the first 48 hours unless something is broken.
7. **Judge only by sales and cost per sale.** No sale after 3 days, or sales above break-even: turn that ad group off and test a new message. 7 days of visitors and zero sales: the product or page is the problem.
8. **Raise budget only in real profit**, in small steps a few days apart.

## Mistakes

- **Being too slow.** Three days of ads teach more than three weeks of polishing.
- **Promising what you can't keep.** "Arrives in 2 days" when it takes 10 brings refunds.
- **Quitting after one bad day.** Ads swing. Follow your rules, not your mood.

## What happened when I tried it

My 2023 notes list "TikTok Dropshipping" as an early try. I have no record of a single sale from it.

On 22.09.2026 I started AI dropshipping again with a partner, 50/50 on profit: Christmas beds for small dogs and cats, sold in the US for $69.99 with free shipping.

The honest result so far: **0 orders** from 01.10.2026 until mid-October. The shop worked, but everything around it got stuck:

- Ad accounts got blocked, and for days we had no working way to run ads.
- Suppliers answered slowly or only partly.
- We are both under 18, so payouts need a parent as account owner.
- We spent too much time on plans and shop fixes instead of getting ads live.

My lesson: dropshipping isn't "build a shop". It's "get a working ad account, make lots of creatives, and test fast with money you can afford to lose". If you are a teen, start with something that needs no ad money, like [reselling](https://lichtersapps.com/ai/reselling.md).
