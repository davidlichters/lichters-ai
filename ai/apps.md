# Building iOS apps: small tools for people who already pay

The honest part: this got much harder. Of 6,488 apps in my searches, about 30 percent were under a year old, and most of those had fewer than 20 ratings. The obvious "tracker for X" ideas are full. My rough estimate from public data: out of 100 new small apps, maybe 2 to 8 make 1,000 dollars a month after about two years.

My rule: only build if competitors clearly make at least around 2,000 dollars a month.

I dropped a beekeeping app because the leading apps made about 24 and 475 dollars a month.

I started building iOS apps in October 2026 with AI (Claude Code, Xcode, SwiftUI). Before writing code I checked over 400 app ideas against App Store search, autocomplete, thousands of reviews and revenue estimates from a short AppTweak trial, and ranked 100 gaps by expected money.

In about ten days we built 14 apps from that list, for example Ton80 (darts scores) and Breedbook (dog breeder log).

The honest result so far: **zero apps in the App Store, zero downloads, zero euros.** Submitting is blocked by the boring adult parts: developer account details, an address, a registered business, bank and tax data. The plan is three apps a week after that, then a check after 60 days.

What I learned already: with AI, building is not the bottleneck. Choosing the niche and the paperwork are the real work.
