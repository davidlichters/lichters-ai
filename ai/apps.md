# Building iOS apps: small tools for people who already pay

I started building iOS apps in October 2026 with AI (Claude Code, Xcode, SwiftUI). Before writing code I checked over 400 app ideas against App Store search, reviews and revenue estimates, and ranked 100 gaps by expected money. I dropped a beekeeping app because the leading apps made about 24 and 475 dollars a month.

In about ten days we built 14 apps from that list, for example Ton80 (darts scores) and Breedbook (dog breeder log).

**Result: zero apps in the App Store, zero downloads, zero euros.** Submitting is blocked by the boring adult parts: developer account details, an address, a registered business, bank and tax data.

**Why it didn't work:** the only reason was that I had very little experience overall. This model can still work today. Just because it didn't work for me back then doesn't mean it can't work now. With my experience today, I'm convinced almost any of these would work for me.
