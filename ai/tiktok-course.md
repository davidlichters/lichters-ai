# TikTok account plus your own course

Grow a TikTok account about something you are really doing, then sell a small guide to the people who watch. It only works if the person on screen is you.

## What it is

You post short videos about one topic, for example getting stronger, learning to code or saving money as a teen. Later you sell your followers something small and digital: a PDF guide, a short video course or a training plan, usually for 10 to 30 euros. Your videos are the shop window, the guide is the product.

## The rule

Only teach what you have done yourself, and only show results that are really yours. No invented life story, no fake numbers. If you have no results yet, your product is your journey, not your success.

## Why

- **People buy from someone they trust.** A made-up persona breaks the moment someone asks a question you can't answer from experience.
- **Fake claims can be illegal.** Promising money or weight loss results that are not real can count as misleading advertising, and platforms ban accounts for it.
- **A fake story traps you.** You can never show your face, your real numbers or your real progress.
- **A real beginner story is already content.** "Day 40 of training at 14, here is what changed" is honest and interesting.

## How to start

1. **Pick one topic you already do every week.** If you stop doing it, the account dies.
2. **Post one honest video a day for 30 days.** What you did, what you learned, what went wrong.
3. **Win the first second.** Start with the most interesting moment, not with "hey guys".
4. **Watch your numbers.** If most people leave in the first two seconds, change the start. If many watch to the end, make more like it.
5. **Repeat what works.** When one video does well, make three more on the same idea with a different first line. A viral video usually brings views for only two or three weeks.
6. **Listen to the comments.** If ten people ask "what should I eat as a skinny teen", that is your first guide.
7. **Make the product small.** One clear PDF or plan that solves one problem, sold through a simple store page. Under 18 you usually need a parent to set up payments.
8. **Say what the product is and what it is not.** "This is the plan I use, it will not make you ripped in 30 days" builds more trust than big promises.

## Mistakes

- **Faking a persona.** The biggest one, see below.
- **Selling "how to make money" without having made money.** The most common fake course online.
- **Quitting after three weeks.** Most honest accounts need months.

## What happened when I tried it

**2023, the fake version.** At 13 I ran a TikTok account called get_rich_today and tried to sell a ChatGPT course on how to make money, from June to mid-July 2023. I had never made money myself. In my own notes I later called it a scam. It reached about 300 followers. Nobody bought it.

**2026, a fake persona again.** I built an Instagram fitness coach character with a weight loss story and income claims that were never mine. It cost me more than it brought in, and I shut it down. With one or two years of patience it might have made money, but I would have been stuck as someone I am not.

**2025, the honest version.** On 18 January 2025 I uploaded my first fitness TikTok as myself, a skinny teen training five times a week. My first sale came on Halloween 2025: one guide for 25 euros. By January 2026 the account had about 18,000 followers. Small money, but real.
