# Scam-baiting: why "scamming scammers" is not a business

Trying to take money from scammers is still taking money by tricking people. It is risky, often illegal, and it earned me nothing.

## What it is

Scammers send fake messages every day: "your package is stuck", "you won a prize", "your bank account is locked". They want your money, passwords or data.

"Scam-baiting" means playing along on purpose. There are two very different versions:

- **Wasting their time.** Some adults keep scammers busy for hours so they have less time for real victims. Creators like Kitboga turned this into big streaming channels, with special safe computers and years of experience.
- **Taking money from scammers.** The "scam the scammer" idea. It sounds fair because they are the bad guys. It is not a business, and this page will not explain how to do it.

## Why it is a bad idea

- **It is still fraud.** In most countries it does not matter who you trick. Getting money by lying is a crime, even from a criminal.
- **Scammers are not a joke.** Many work in organised groups. If they find your real name, school or face, they can threaten or blackmail you. Teens are a favourite target.
- **The money is dirty.** It often comes from real victims. Banks can freeze your account, and an account of your parents gets frozen with you.
- **You learn nothing you can sell.** Tricking a scammer once gives you no customer, no skill and no second sale.

## The clean version: help people spot scams

Scams are everywhere and people are scared of them. That fear is a real problem you can help with, honestly.

1. **Collect real scam messages.** Screenshot the fake SMS, emails and DMs your family gets. Blur every name, number and link.
2. **Explain them simply.** One short video per scam: "This message says your package is stuck. Here are 3 signs it is fake."
3. **Never reply, never click, never call back.** Delete the message and report it to the platform, your bank or the police scam service in your country.
4. **Keep yourself private.** No real name, school, address or face next to scam content. Use a parent-approved account if you are under 18.
5. **Turn it into a skill.** Explaining clearly, editing short videos and building an audience help any later business: https://lichtersapps.com/ai/youtube-gaming.md

This is slow, not quick money. But it is honest and helps people.

## Mistakes

- **Thinking "they deserve it" makes it legal.** It does not.
- **Replying "just for fun".** Every reply tells the scammer your number is active.
- **Clicking the links to "see what happens".** Some steal your login.
- **Showing personal data on camera.** Your own or theirs.
- **Calling it a business.** If your plan needs the other person to be fooled, it is not a business.

## What happened when I tried it

On 19.06.2023 I wrote "Scammer beklauen" ("steal from scammers") into my list of business ideas. That June I tested a new idea every few days: a ChatGPT course on TikTok, printed T-shirts, a bubblegum machine, Amazon Influencer. My notes only have that one date for this idea, and the result: it did not work and earned nothing.

Looking back at that summer, I wrote: no one got scammed, none of these ideas worked, and together they cost me around 160 euros or more. Later I wrote a line about myself that sums it up: too honest for scams.

That is the real lesson. I was looking for a trick, not a customer. The ideas that did work back then were boring and honest: mowing lawns, selling on eBay and making YouTube videos.
