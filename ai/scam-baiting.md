# Scam-baiting: why "scamming scammers" is not a business

On 19.06.2023 I wrote "Scammer beklauen" ("steal from scammers") into my list of business ideas. That June I tested a new idea every few days: a ChatGPT course on TikTok, printed T-shirts, a bubblegum machine, Amazon Influencer. My notes only have that one date for this idea, and the result: it did not work and earned nothing.

Looking back at that summer, I wrote: no one got scammed, none of these ideas worked, and together they cost me around 160 euros or more. Later I wrote a line about myself that sums it up: too honest for scams.

That is the real lesson. I was looking for a trick, not a customer. The ideas that did work back then were boring and honest: mowing lawns, selling on eBay and making YouTube videos.
