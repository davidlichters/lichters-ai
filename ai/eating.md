# Eating

Five handfuls of fruit and vegetables every day, and water instead of soda. It is the simplest habit on the list, and it pays off for decades.

## The rule

Tick **e** when you ate 5+ handfuls of fruit and vegetables today and drank water instead of soda.

- One handful is about what fits in your hand: an apple, a banana, a carrot, a handful of berries, a bowl of salad, a side of cooked vegetables.
- Fresh, frozen and canned all count. Potatoes and fries do not.
- Juice counts once at most. Smoothies too.
- Soda, energy drinks and iced tea with sugar break the tick. Water, sparkling water and unsweetened tea are fine.

## Why

- **Aune and others, 2017, International Journal of Epidemiology.** A review of 95 studies: people who ate about 800 g of fruit and vegetables a day had a roughly 31% lower risk of dying early than people who ate very little. 800 g is about 10 handfuls, so 5+ is the start, not the top. https://doi.org/10.1093/ije/dyw319

Be honest about what that means: these studies observe people, they do not prove cause. People who eat a lot of vegetables may also sleep, train and smoke differently. The link is still large and steady across many countries. For happiness the gain comes mostly through more healthy years.

Water instead of soda is judgment: it was not tested in that study. More on the research: https://lichtersapps.com/ai/research.md

## How to start (at 13)

1. **One handful at every meal.** Breakfast, lunch, dinner: that is already 3.
2. **Two snacks you can grab.** An apple and a carrot in your school bag, packed the night before.
3. **Ask whoever buys the food.** "Can we get more fruit and frozen vegetables?" Most parents say yes to that.
4. **Keep a water bottle with you.** Fill it before school and before training.
5. **Count with your fingers.** Five fingers, five handfuls. Tick when you reach the last one.

## Mistakes

- **Waiting for dinner.** If you have 1 handful at 18:00, you will not get to 5. Start at breakfast.
- **"Juice counts five times."** It doesn't. It has the sugar without the fibre.
- **Diet rules and calorie counting.** This habit is about adding food, not cutting it. If eating or your weight worries you, talk to a parent or doctor.
- **Energy drinks before training.** Lots of sugar and caffeine, not made for teens. Water is enough.
